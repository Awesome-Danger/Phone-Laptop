The Gospel" translates to "The Good News." 
The Gospel is the message to the whole world--including *every* nation and individual--that we have been given the opportunity to be free. Our sins, the bad things we do, they separate us from God, our Father and King, who loves us. The payment of sin is death. We would all be condemned if God hadn't sent his son Jesus to die on the cross as a sacrifice for our sins. This is what we know as "The Good News." He was the sacrifice that paid the debt of everyone in the Old Testament, and everyone today. We did nothing to deserve it (in fact, we did just about everything *not* to), and nothing we can do nothing to earn it. However, what we *can* do--and are called to do--is to live worthy of his sacrifice; to honor it with our lives/lifestyles/actions. Through Jesus' death, or sin dies; and through his resurrection after he died on the cross, we live. Belief, and love for God, aided by our obedience to Him in the form of action, makes a complete, and whole faith. Salvation is given to us by God's grace and we receive it through our faith. The gift comes to us in the form of the Holy Spirit. Our job as Christians is to go through this world spreading that Gospel to everyone, which the Holy Spirit helps us to do.
