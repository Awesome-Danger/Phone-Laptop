- This eye is made of unbreakable obsidian with a jade iris and can heal the mind of anybody that looks into it no matter how broken, or beaten; little, or large. This works on all mammals, but not bugs. Animals of all kinds are pacified by the presence of the eye, and will often accompany the wielder if they so wish.
