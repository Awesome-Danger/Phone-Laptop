When attuned to this glass eye, the unseen world is revealed to you (User can see hidden/invisible objects, +3 bonus to “spot-hidden” roles)

- [[Corruption]] is seen as a *dark blue fire*
- invisible objects and creatures are seen as *blue glass*
- friendly otherworldly creatures are seen to have *yellow/golden fire*
- hostile otherworldly creatures are seen to have *dark red fire*
- neutral otherworldly creatures are seen to have *white fire*
