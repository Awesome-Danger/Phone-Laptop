
*A gold ring with a large, cut, red sapphire that contains a pool of swirling energy within it.*

Allows the player to have a pool of health they can use to heal at any point (max = 40HP). The ring comes with a half-full pool, but never regenerates the pool by itself; all HP must be added manually by the player while conscious. **In combat, healing takes a reaction**.
