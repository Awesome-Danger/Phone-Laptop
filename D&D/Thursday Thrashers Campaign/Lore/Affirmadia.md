
>Most people in Affirmadia don't know that the large blue flame at the center of the city is not actually a strand. 
>- Affirmadia has a warped understanding of what the Blue Flame is. To them, the [[Blue Flame of Affirmadia]] is a gift from the gods, and what makes Affirmadia livable.
>- *In reality, it was a point of defense placed during the war between the [[Corruption]], and the Resistance used for redundancy. Corrupted foes weren't able to see through the blizzard that it created, and would often end up freezing to death before they reached Affirmadia. Affirmadia was the headquarters of the Resistance. The high mountains made it difficult for enemies to make much progress even if they went through the storm, it would tire them greatly to traverse up and through the mountainous landscape.*
