[Bjorn hurls Ulfgar through the light with all his might, and stays behind. As the light fades, he's left at the monument that once shifted, and moved, but is now still. Bjorn decides to take a short rest to heal, then heads off.]
[The giant spiders are still pinned to trees, and bodies hanging from the branches, and run through on spears, decomposing heads on pikes; the road seemingly never ends. Bjorn hears the armies fighting in the distance, and decides to check it out. He witnesses a bunch of magicians in green clothing casting spells to fight off the giant spiders, and other creatures he can't quite make out. The creatures are attacking the foot soldiers; some spiders are getting speared through and pinned to the trees, while others are feasting on the men they take down.] 
From behind, Bjorn hears a voice call out to him saying, *"Why are you here?"* 
Bjorn replies, *"I want to find out what this place is... I want information about this place. It might be able to help us fight off the king!"*
When Bjorn turns around, he doesn't see anybody. *"Where are you? Who are you? What are **you** doing here?"*
The voice replies, *"My name is Ranii. I am the consciousness you and your friends have awakened, and currently is bound to your friend... Theadosia. However, you have only half awakened me... Your intuition is most impressive. You found out that I needed to feed off of vitality to be awakened enough to let you out of here."*
Bjorn asks, *"What is this place?"*
*"Don't you realize already? This is a memory from my own mind. The battle you witness now is the Great Rebellion. As you see now, we are fighting against these evil creatures that once ruled the world... We called it The Corruption."*
Bjorn asks, *"What is that? What does this have to do with **our** predicament with the king?"*
*"Everything! You see, I believe the king has gained the power of The Corruption! We--I had sealed it away with the help of Belgabad, your leader."*
*"Belgabad fought with you? I have never heard of you-"*
*"Yes, that is because he was never supposed to. It never would have helped, unless I came into danger. That is why **you** are here. He sent you to help me. As you very well know, your Creed runs on a need-to-know basis. You weren't told why you were sent out here, but only that it's for the sake of the Creed, and the world. That is only half the truth..."* 
Bjorn, panicked, says, *"You can't be serious! You must be lying-"*
