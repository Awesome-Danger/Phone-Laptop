## plot concepts
after having a bunch of spells cast on him that he didn't know the use for, but allowed. A decade of his life seemingly disappeared, which he has no memory of. Now he knows he is on a quest, but doesn't know exactly what it is, or why.

### the quest
He has been put up to the task of being a spy for a certain kingdom/house, and has been for years, but as for the mission he was put on, he doesn't realize he is supposed to be a spy.
Cairn is spying on a certain group/organization/person to gather information. He has had his memory erased, so that even if he was tortured, he *couldn't* spill any information on who he's spying for. However, he had died for ten years, and was only resurrected recently, thus not remembering the past 10 years.
