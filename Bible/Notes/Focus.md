anatomy of focus
1. strip away
	- discard every encumbering weight (Heb12:4)
2. eyes ahead
3. press forward

Aphorao
- look away to see one (Heb12:2)
	- actively block peripheral noise to maintain central alignment
	- to focus on the waves, you need to tune out everything else

Hupomone
- steadfast endurance (Heb12:1)
	- focus requires the structural grit to outlast the storm
	- you'll go through difficulty, but you need to have faith and endurance

Skopos
- the distant mark (philippians3:14)
	- your daily actions must align with your ultimate objective
