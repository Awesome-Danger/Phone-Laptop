People tend to believe that Christianity is about rules. What would make them believe this?

- Christians tend to become legalistic about Christianity
- Tend to point out all the ways people are failing in life
	- tends to turn people off from the Bible

What do we think about the Bible being a bunch of rules? Do we think it's a bunch of rules?
- it's not about **rules**, it's about values: specifically **love**

> [!bible]+ [Matthew 23:23-24 - ESV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+23:23-24&version=esv)
> <sup>23</sup> “Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you tithe mint and dill and cumin, and have neglected the weightier matters of the law: justice and mercy and faithfulness. These you ought to have done, without neglecting the others.
> <sup>24</sup> You blind guides, straining out a gnat and swallowing a camel!

- the scribes were **very** well acquainted with the scriptures, as they constantly rewrote the scriptures word for word.
- the pharisees studied and taught the law (and also had a hand in enforcing the law (in a way that it's not supposed to be)). They would enforce the letter of the Law (strict adherence), rather than the **intent** of the Law (to worship God)
	- they thought of the Law as a list of "dos" and "don'ts"

- They're tithing (even their expensive spices), but Jesus calls them out on neglecting the most important stuff... 
	- why do they give a tithe? because it belongs to God, and to acknowledge it's God's, and that they themselves are God's. They were supposed to honor and recognize God's faithfulness.
- Jesus calls them out on neglecting the most important stuff: the things that are closer to God's heart: justice, mercy and faithfulness.

- Jesus is not telling them to stop doing what the Law is telling them to do, but to acknowledge why they're doing it.

> [!bible]+ [Matthew 23:24 - ESV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+23:24&version=esv)
> <sup>24</sup> You blind guides, straining out a gnat and swallowing a camel!

- Jesus calls them blind guides, because they have become false teachers
- Jesus uses a creative phrase of "straining out a gnat and swallowing a camel" to show that they are holding the insignificant things over people's heads, yet missing the giant thing right there

> [!bible]+ [Matthew 23:25 - ESV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+23:25&version=esv)
> <sup>25</sup> “Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you clean the outside of the cup and the plate, but inside they are full of greed and self-indulgence.

Jesus calls them out as being greedy, but wait a second... these are upstanding citizens, who are giving great tithes, and upholding the law to the extent they see it. How are they greedy?
- they're stealing attention from God to themselves.
- they're polishing the outside of their dish like a man obsessed with his car, but leave the inside filthy.

> [!bible]+ [Matthew 23:26 - ESV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+23:26&version=esv)
> <sup>26</sup> You blind Pharisee! First clean the inside of the cup and the plate, that the outside also may be clean.

- Jesus points out that the relationship with God is to clean the inside first so the outside will become clean...

> [!bible]+ [Matthew 23:27 - ESV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+23:27&version=esv)
> <sup>27</sup> “Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you are like whitewashed tombs, which outwardly appear beautiful, but within are full of dead people's bones and all uncleanness.

- but the pharisees polish only the outside, leaving the inside to rot. inside the tomb is death and decay.
- and in case they don't get it yet:

> [!bible]+ [Matthew 23:28 - ESV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+23:28&version=esv)
> <sup>28</sup> So you also outwardly appear righteous to others, but within you are full of hypocrisy and lawlessness.

introducing this topic here: the idea that the Bible is just a list of rules.

- we cannot argue with the fact that the Law of Moses is a list of rules, and there are things we are told to avoid, if it's only a bunch of rules, it's missing God.
