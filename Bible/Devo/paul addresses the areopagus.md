*what do you notice about Paul in this passage?*


- Paul has done his homework on his audience
- uses their own literature for God's purposes (copy-paste, but instead of Zeus, it's God)
- he levels with them about things they can understand
- doesn't "put them down" or their beliefs

- acknowledges their efforts
- wants to redirect them to the right path
- **he never mentions sin outright**
	- he does mention "repentance" for ignorance, a thing they can understand
- he was proclaiming in Athens "*every day*"
	- proclaiming the gospel is not a one-off, or "one and done" thing, but a continued **effort**
	- Jesus and his followers do this often. Overlooked
