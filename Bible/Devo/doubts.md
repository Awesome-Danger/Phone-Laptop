
churches and religious environments often retain an environment that discourages asking questions, questioning faith, or **having doubts**. 

letting doubts grow, and flourish after they've been conceived is a terrible thing, and that's the bad part about doubts. doubts in of themselves aren't necessarily bad, but it's letting them sit, grow, flourish, and kill you that is the worst part.

we need to encourage asking questions, and not just mindlessly accepting things as dogma. we are encouraged in the Bible to ask questions, and test our faith.

> [!bible]+ James 1:2-3 - ESV
> <sup>2</sup> Count it all joy, my brothers,  when you meet trials of various kinds,
> <sup>3</sup> for you know that the testing of your faith produces steadfastness.

> [!bible]+ Ephesians 4:14-15 - ESV
> <sup>14</sup> so that we may no longer be children, tossed to and fro by the waves and carried about by every wind of doctrine, by human cunning, by craftiness in deceitful schemes.
> <sup>15</sup> Rather, speaking the truth in love, we are to grow up in every way into him who is the head, into Christ,

> [!bible]+ Ephesians 6:13 - ESV
> <sup>13</sup> Therefore take up the whole armor of God, that you may be able to withstand in the evil day, and having done all, to stand firm.

> [!bible]+ Colossians 2:8 - ESV
> <sup>8</sup> See to it that no one takes you captive by philosophy and empty deceit, according to human tradition, according to the elemental spirits  of the world, and not according to Christ.
