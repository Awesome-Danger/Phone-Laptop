The Corruption was a force of evil from near the dawn of time, which was sealed away. [[Echoes of Corruption]] are still around the world today.

He is the mighty red dragon Sharruth that was entombed on the sea floor, creating Stormwreck Isle.

> "The island was formed in ancient times by volcanic activity that was caused when the mighty red dragon Sharruth was entombed on the sea floor by a group of three [metallic dragons](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Metallic_dragon "Metallic dragon"): the [bronze dragon](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Bronze_dragon "Bronze dragon") [Astalagan](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Astalagan "Astalagan"), the gold dragon [Clyssavar](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Clyssavar "Clyssavar"), and the [brass dragon](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Brass_dragon "Brass dragon") [Turadaer](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Turadaer "Turadaer"). Ever since, the island was a beacon to dragons, and thus hosted many battles in the never-ending struggle between metallic and [chromatic dragons](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Chromatic_dragon "Chromatic dragon"). This, in turn, caused the island to become suffused with the magic of the slain dragons."
