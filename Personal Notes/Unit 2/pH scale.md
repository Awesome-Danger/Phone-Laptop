(basically "per Hydrogen")
- Determined by amount of hydrogen in a substance
- Scale ranges from 0 to 14 with 7 being neutral
- 0-6.9 is an acid
- 7.1 to 14 is a base
- Acids have more H cations and bases have more OH anions
- Based on a logarithmic scale of 10; each step is on a scale of ten
- Human body tries to maintain between 7.2 to 7.6; blood is around 7.4 (7.35 - 7.45)
- It is necessary for living things to maintain pH

- water used to be called neutral, and perhaps filtered water is, but due to pollution, it is acidic.
- water is the only substance that is  neutral that's produced naturally, if it's not tainted by the atmosphere

### Why is controlling pH important?
- Chemical reactions in the body require enzymes to work properly
- Enzymes function best between pH 6 and pH 8
- Enzymes are proteins, therefore, they will denature when at the inappropriate pH
- Buffers in the body work to maintain these optimum pH’s
- Calcium carbonate and carbonic acid are examples

when you cook an egg in a pan, and the egg turns white, that's the proteins denaturing. same thing happens in the body if it's blood becomes acidodic
