
7 - I agree that worship to our God from all nations is the ultimate goal of our lives, and that missions is only the means by which we will accomplish that. It's imprortant to know *why* we're doing what we're doing, so that it's not mindless, and done with passionate love, rather than blind passion. Love of the lost is indeed something that isn't usually a warm fuzzy feeling, but rather the way in which you act towards them. Love can often be misconstrued as a feeling, when it's not what that is at all. True love endures through anger; meaning it's still present. God is angry with Israel **all** the time, but He still loves them. Even for Jesus, who was love made manifest (1 John 4:9). If love were a feeling, we couldn't call God all loving without taking away his justice and wrath, which takes away the whole meaning behind grace and mercy. This love is the kind that only His Spirit can give.
19 - I feel like an article explaining that we need to do what Jesus says in a specific verse is overkill... however, I think there is good that comes out of distinguishing the difference between political nations, individuals, and all *types* of people groups. Teaching to individuals spreads it to them, possibly their family, and their friends. All people groups is specific enough to where people of all ages, nations, locations, educations, and situations are all familiar with the gospel, and are familiar enough to be able to spread the hope of it to each other from the Spirit. Whether you're in the slums, middle class, or the 1%, we all will know the LORD. This is best represented through "all people groups/peoples" rather than all nations. In any case, though, our mission is to preach it to all *Homo sapiens*.
