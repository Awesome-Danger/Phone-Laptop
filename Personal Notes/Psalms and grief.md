**the psalms are unabashedly, unapologetically emotional.** they don't ask, they just do it. They use strong language as well.

they call for violent revenge against enemies,

celebration, and deep sadness in loss. 

Being a New Testament Christian doesn't exclude you
Jesus is the Word made flesh

discussing the psalms as poetry, they arouse emotions, imagination, and imagery.

they insist that they have emotions and must be expressed, without it being wrong or immoral.

**The Psalms do not weaponize emotions.** they are expressed to God, not other people. More than just allowing it, God insists that we come to Him with our grief.
