Red sparkly pipe cleaner
Help maintain cell shape and move materials inside the cell.

Hollow, tube-shaped fibers.

### [[cytoskeleton|microfilaments]]
Green pipe cleaner
Help the cell keep its shape and help it move materials.
[[Alzheimer's Disease]] and [[Huntington’s Disease]], and other neurodegenerative diseases
Very thin strands.

### [[cytoskeleton|centrioles]]
Yellow pipe cleaner
Help organize microtubules during cell division.
Microcephaly (some forms are linked to centrosome-related defects)
- birth defect $\to$ baby's head is smaller than it's supposed to be, because the brain has stopped growing either during pregnancy, or after birth.
Small cylinders made of microtubules.

### [[mitochondria]]
Puffy yellow pipe cleaner
Make energy for the cell.
MELAS syndrome (mitochondrial encephalomyopathy, lactic acidosis, and stroke-like episodes)
- seizures, epilepsy, anorexia, short stature, impaired muscle coordination, etc.
Small ovals with folded inner membranes.
