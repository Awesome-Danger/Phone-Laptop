God wants you to be loyal, but we don't like to obey anybody but ourselves. Like children, we deny the authority of those that God has given authority to, such as our parents, elders, and sometimes even our government; and thus we deny the authority of God, who has placed them there (though in the case of a wicked kingdom/government, God does often bring destruction on them through people).
