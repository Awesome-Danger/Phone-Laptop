Being a Christian involves maintaining the relationship. **It's not passive.** 
	It doesn't matter what you say, or what I say, it matters what God says. Does God say that you don't need to put any work into the relationship? Or does He Himself explicitly state what we need to do? God's *love* is unconditional, *not His salvation.* I think we get confused by that a lot, because it we like to think that God will save everyone He loves, but God loves the world (John 3:16). Is the whole world going to be saved? Absolutely not! Not everyone who cried "lord, lord" will be saved. Jesus will say to them "I never knew you."

If you're in a marriage, and your spouse has a severe drinking problem, but you're going to work it through with them. How long before your patience wains? Let's say he hasn't even tried to get any better, and has only gotten worse; he's started beating, and abusing you; he screams at you, but afterwards always buys you flowers, and apologizes. Is that a healthy relationship? At what point do you think you would leave? Probably when you very clearly realize that this relationship is never going to change, or get better. So let's say you're ready to leave him, but he begs you to stay, and promises that he'll change. let's say you stay, and tell him that you will only stay if he puts genuine effort in to change his ways, and recover. So you stay with him, and he goes to an AA meeting. He comes back, and says that he feels great, and he's clean now. Do you believe him? You stick with him, and he hasn't gone to any more meetings, and gets worse. You tell him that he should go to another meeting, and attend regularly, but he ignores you, and dean't go; he continues to get worse and worse, and when you say that if he doesn't get help, you're gonna leave him, he say fine, but doesn't do anything. How long would you stick around to wait if he gets better? He doesn't go to any meetings, and you've had it with him. He keeps saying he loves you, as if that's going to make things magically better, and doesn't do anything to improve the situation. He ignores you, but at least he doesn't really beat you anymore. But you've had it, and you leave him. Who walked out on the relationship? Who ended the relationship? Who killed/destroyed the relationship? *Who is the reason as to why the relationship didn't work out?* Is it yours, because you decided that it was done; or is it his, because he never maintained the relationship with you?
	We must maintain the relationship for there to be one. Grace is given to us as a gift that we can't ever pay back, or earn, or truly deserve; but salvation doesn't stop at accepting the gift. We need to live worthy of that gift he has graciously given to us.
